**ORIGINAL SOURCE TEST EVIDENCE (claims, not reproduced):** the May 2021 author reports testing from **January 1, 2007 to December 31, 2020**, using AmiBroker and Norgate Data for cash S&P 500 constituent stocks on *daily* inputs, with selections monthly. The author explicitly compares raw closing-price rate of change, ROC on EMA, ROC divided by historical volatility, ROC multiplied by historical volatility, and volatility alone across **63, 126, 189, 252** trading-day lookbacks and optional 5-stock/no-stock-SMA200 variants mentioned in a gated spreadsheet. The author says ROC/HV failed to improve CAGR-like compounded annual return (CAR) and Sharpe, while multiplying ROC by HV raised **CAR about 25% and Sharpe about 22%**, with slightly better drawdown/Ulcer Index, relative to the article's comparison aggregates. The June 2021 follow-up says high price, ROC/ATR%, and Kaufman Efficiency Ratio times ROC had some potential but did not outperform ROC times HV overall. **Those relative results are author claims, not a verified 63-day single-variant result.** Full table, exact trade count, absolute CAR, maximum drawdown, calendar-year losses, per-share commission, spreads, slippage, and exact trials selected are **UNKNOWN from the accessible public text**; the author supplies fuller records through an email-gated spreadsheet, not inspected here.

## ORIGINAL_AUTHOR_RULES: do not silently mutate

1. On the **last exchange trading day of each month**, work from historical **S&P 500 constituents** only.
2. Require selected stock's **daily close above its 200-day simple moving average**, and **SPX daily close above its own 200-day simple moving average**.
3. Rank eligible stock securities **descending**, using four disclosed lookbacks in the author's comparisons: 63, 126, 189, and 252 daily trading sessions. The explicitly printed 63-day expression is `ROC(C,63)*HV(63)`. Author's AmiBroker HV formula is `StDev(log(C/Ref(C,-1)),len)*(252^.5)*100`. Standard ROC is percentage change of C over the given lookback; the volatility function is an annualized standard deviation of log-close returns. The 252 here is **annualization days**, *not* the HV lookback for the 63-day example.
4. **Buy the 10 highest-ranked names at the open on the following trading day.** The source's sell line is **sell all at the open on the first trading day of the month**; in monthly rotation context this appears to mean **the subsequent month's** first open. The public text does not supply explicit multi-month order code; preserve the ambiguity until author-code parity.
5. **Source does not publicly specify weighting, leverage, fill eligibility, auction participation, position-level stop, trade cap, exact settlement effects, or 2021 transaction costs.** Do not claim an author-prescribed equal-weight/zero-fee model. Related 2013 backtest assumed $0.01/share commission; transferring that number into the **2021 experiment** would be unjustified.

The true original product is **individual US listed equities**, not an index future, cash index alone, S&P 500 ETF, CFD, or crypto perpetual. SPX is a **market-state filter**; a SPY total-return series may be a benchmark, never a substitute for eligible stock-level execution.

## LOCAL_ADAPTATION (declared, not author-verified)

Design just **one frozen research configuration** derived from the author's printed example: 63-session ROC times 63-session HV, same 200-day filters, top 10 once per exchange calendar month, unlevered **equal-dollar 10% positions** when fully eligible, and otherwise cash. Cash is held when the SPX filter is false. Use **identical** clock, universe, sizing, and fill/cost model for an **ROC63-only top-10 baseline**. This is a proposed *scientific comparison*, not a claim the author selected 63 days as his unique global winner. The author's reported uplift came after testing multiple alternatives; **full family trial accounting is unavailable**.

All proposed indicators use strictly completed closes from the signal month end, with the signal declared **after** close publication. At least 200 completed sessions are required for each SMA200; ROC63 uses 64 completed closes, and HV63 uses 63 successive completed close-to-close log returns. Check sample-vs-population StDev convention against AmiBroker rather than silently declaring identical values. Use PIT identifier- and split-aware stock prices; do not use future-adjusted company universes. Rebalance on the **next month's first trading day**, with the earliest *orderable* auction execution if authenticated venue rules show the order could have been submitted, or the first usable quote after the open. This is deliberately stricter than an idealized historical opening print.

**Market clocks:** each signal and constituent eligibility uses **America/New_York** exchange calendar dates; UTC storage must honor NY DST and exchange holidays/early closes. Opening-fills require correct NYSE/Nasdaq listing venue and official auction/quote timing. No 16:00 close signal may trigger a 16:00 fill. Changes in index membership announced but not yet effective are not retroactively included. For delisted or halted securities, preserve failures and distributions, not surviving tickers only.

**Transaction cost plan:** source effective-dated per-share commissions and regulatory fees, stock-specific half-spreads, opening-auction or post-open slippage, size/ADV impact, dividend/corporate-action cashflows, cash interest and any actual financing charges. Consider predeclared 1x/2x/3x sourced-cost stress *after* the necessary data are qualified. No made-up bps hurdle, no futures funding, no assumption of zero open gap slippage.

## Economic claim and independent challenge

**HYPOTHESIS:** In listed equities with both local and broad-market upward trend, multiplying positive momentum by stock-specific realized volatility selects names where major public information shocks and delayed portfolio adjustments keep price discovery going into the subsequent month. **Potential payer:** slower rebalancers or investors needing urgent risk transfer. This counterparty is **INFERENCE**, not identified empirically in the author blog.

**Strongest adverse evidence:** the author's own volatility-normalized alternative `ROC/HV` was a bust on CAR and Sharpe, showing ranking performance can swing under an arbitrary monotonic volatility transformation. ROC*HV mechanically loads more exposure into high-vol names during risk-on periods. High volatility may proxy for high beta, recent tech leadership, short squeezes and crashes rather than independent selection skill. The **63-day configuration was one of many tested** with no disclosed per-variant out-of-sample trade list or selection-adjusted confidence. The 2013 **different** rotation study describes **up to 89%** maximum drawdown in a variant. That is a *contextual fragility warning*, **not** a claimed 2021 ROC*HV drawdown. In a **2017 comment** on the older study, a reader claimed his alternative 1957-forward rotation test lagged holding SPY; neither the comment nor its differing rules is an independent replication of the 2021 variant.

**Selection and survivorship risks:** the author's code and Norgate data are not public; author says Norgate and elsewhere confirms historical index membership was used in his older research, but that does **not** verify *our* dataset or the 2021 exact security master. Using today's S&P 500 constituents in a 2007 test is disallowed. Stocks that disappeared after negative returns and corporate-action discontinuities must not be dropped. Source result has obvious market- and parameter-selection and later narrative-selection exposure; no evidence of an independent 2021-2026 update is asserted.

## Proposed scientific protocol (design only; NO Phase 3/4 execution)

**Data gate first:** inspect only data *metadata* under separately authorized follow-up; establish effective-dated S&P 500 constituent and security-identity history, split/dividend/delisting handling, SPX daily closes, qualifying exchange sessions, and executable primary-market auction or post-open quotes. No metadata inspection of the stock universe, no quality test, and no market returns were performed in this run. Treat feasibility and cost basis as unresolved.

**Phase 3: actual cross-sectional factor diagnostic.** Because the hypothesis ranks multiple stocks each month, use the project's native `ml4t.diagnostic` signal-evaluation method after installed signature verification. Evaluate month-by-month **Spearman rank IC** of `ROC63*HV63` against future **next first-open to following first-open** cash-stock returns, among **exactly identical point-in-time eligible constituents**. Compute the paired monthly **incremental IC** versus `ROC63`; report sample size, monthly variability, chronologically separated development regimes, paired dependence-aware confidence uncertainty, and the tail-oriented top-10 spread (without pretending IC is portfolio P&L). Freeze label timing and family trial accounting before seeing outcomes. Include controls for beta, sector and size and a simple rank baseline. This is a proposed native diagnostic, **not a reported result**.

**Primary preregistered falsifier:** if the paired incremental monthly cross-sectional Spearman IC for ROC63*HV63 relative to ROC63 is not positive in eligible chronological development data after the predeclared uncertainty/multiple-comparison treatment, reject the claim that volatility multiplication improves ranking. Positive absolute market returns alone do not rescue that incrementality failure. Phase 4 full native event-driven simulation requires a separately recorded Phase 3 decision and authorization; even a positive Phase 3 is not backtest approval, and no protected final holdout is touched.

**Mechanical parity tests to specify before implementation:** independent indicator oracle vs AmiBroker printed formulas; 63-return HV 64-close warmup; session shift invariant at DST, holiday and early close; future-data append invariant; historical membership effective dates and delisted-stock accounting; same-clock comparator construction; auction-cutoff / quote availability; overnight gaps, spreads and open volume/impact; documented corporate actions and dividends. All library implementations and parameter signatures must be inspected later, not invented here.
