**SOURCE_FACT** — Mohamed Saidane and Ezzeddine Ben Mohamed, “Forecasting Regime-Dependent Tail Risk in Digital Asset Portfolios With a Mixture of Hidden Markov Factor Analyzers,” *Journal of Forecasting* 45(6), 2735–2759 (first published 16 April 2026), DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/for.70159. The available publisher-indexed abstract describes a **six-cryptocurrency portfolio**, a hidden Markov chain, latent factor model, regime-specific Gaussian mixtures, expectation–maximization fitting, and probabilistic forecast VaR/expected shortfall generated by Monte Carlo. It assesses forecasts under the Patton–Ziegel–Chen joint loss and reports a risk-averse allocation assessment after transaction costs.

**AUTHOR_TEST_UNVERIFIED** — Authors report better out-of-sample joint VaR–ES loss than competing models, satisfactory Basel III tests at a 2.5% tail level, and after-cost performance for a risk-averse strategy. No local reproduction, independent model audit, precise net performance or cost schedule was obtained. A 2.5% tail level is **not** a trading horizon.

**LOCAL_INFERENCE** — The prospective ML advantage comes from learning evolving correlations and changing loss-tail shapes, potentially capturing vulnerabilities a static covariance or unconditional empirical VaR misses. This is not evidence that an HMM predicts returns better than a momentum rule.
