# Time-varying directed cryptocurrency return network — SOURCE DRAFT ONLY

**Status:** THESIS_CAPTURED; descriptive source capture; not executable ATS handoff or research-stage approval.

**Original:** Shubhangam Shukla, Mahesh Peyyala and Abhijit Chakraborty, *Time-dependent weighted directed networks of cryptocurrency interaction from high-frequency returns*, arXiv:2606.25466, submitted 24 June 2026, revised v3 25 August 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25466

**Source fact:** High-frequency crypto returns (2020–2025) are used to construct directed/weighted networks from significant Granger relationships. Nodes are ranked by outgoing connection strength. The source abstract reports a changing transmission hierarchy and describes Ethereum as an influential transmitter, with Bitcoin declining in relative rank. This is **observational structure**, not evidence of tradeable lead-lag profit.

**Explicit missing original rules:** Exact cryptocurrency symbols, venues and timestamp alignment; graph lookback and p-value filtering; trading signal directions, long and short entry thresholds, portfolio size/weights, rebalance and exits, per-leg fees, financing, and causal information-publication timing all **UNKNOWN**. No long/short basket is specified in the original abstract.

**Independent, non-author proposal:** Convert past-only Granger transmitter/receiver information to lagged cross-sectional rankings and test for predictability, with an explicit null comparison. This is only an **ADAPTATION PROPOSAL**, not an author claim, and was NOT evaluated.

**Falsification / adverse:** Networks estimated with the entire history, contemporaneous causality tests or selected hindsight leaders can easily create leakage and multiple-testing bias. Significant Granger edges do not imply fillable returns after turnover and costs. Small-cap coverage, shortability and prop account rules **UNKNOWN**.
