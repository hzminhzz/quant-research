## 2. Original author's SPY experiment — preserve exact product and meaning

**VERIFIED — source statement:** A large gap lower means today's SPY regular-session open is at least **0.75% below the previous cash-session close**. Hanna analyzed purchase at the **opening price** and sale at the **same session's closing price**, conditional on trading below or above the **200-session simple moving average**. The article presents no ATR, stop, take-profit, weekday limitation, VIX gate, leverage, short leg or optimizer. Use the below-SMA subgroup as the fixed original pattern. The source does not establish positive net profits at executable prices.

**VERIFIED — supporting definition:** Hanna's April 22, 2008 mid-sized gap study explicitly defines a downtrend as the *close below the 200-day moving average*. A causal interpretation for a next-day opening trade is **the previous completed cash-session close against the SMA200 of completed daily closes**. This contextual cross-source definition resolves some ambiguity, but we have no code-level parity proof of the January sample classifications.

**VERIFIED — original source claim, not independent PnL:** The January 2008 article reports **55 winning trades in 105** under-SMA events (52.4%) and **over 34.8% 'net gain' summed/reported across events**.
