# Asset-specific downside-risk premium in crypto

**SOURCE_CAPTURE_ONLY — observational descriptive hypothesis, NOT a ready-made trading rule.**

**Original paper:** Wei Zhang, Yi Li, Xiong Xiong, Pengfei Wang, [*Downside risk and the cross-section of cryptocurrency returns* (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbankfin.2021.106246), Journal of Banking & Finance 133, 106246. Verified abstract at [RePEc](https://econpapers.repec.org/article/eeejbfina/v_3a133_3ay_3a2021_3ai_3ac_3as0378426621002053.htm).

**Source finding:** Authors report a positive cross-sectional link between per-coin downside risk and future returns in portfolio analysis and regressions, and consider the risk premium versus limits-to-arbitrage interpretation. They also inspect upside risk and temporal persistence. This abstract alone DOES NOT reveal the precise downside measure, rank/lookback, portfolio trade legs, weights, venue or costs. A long high-risk/short low-risk basket would be a new adaptation, not an author-established rule, so no executable spec is asserted.

**UNVERIFIED:** all author statistical claims and implementation costs. No local testing. Economic alternative: concentrated thin/illiquid names, survivorship or beta exposures drive raw association. [Fieberg et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.irfa.2024.103218) provide serious contrary tradeability findings on crypto factors.

**Registry distinction:** idiosyncratic asset downside-risk premium is conceptually different from relative/downside market beta in existing entries; exact computation could overlap after reading original methodology and must be deduplicated then.

**Transfer UNKNOWN:** prop coins and shorts, gross/net exposure, margin, financing, overnight/weekend behavior, max losses. No funding signal, backtest, runner, protected reads or orders.
