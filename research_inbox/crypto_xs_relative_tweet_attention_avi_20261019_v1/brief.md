# crypto_xs_relative_tweet_attention_avi_20261019_v1 — relative tweet attention as a cross-sectional research signal

**Status:** descriptive source capture only. `HANDOFF_VALID_UNVERIFIED`, if returned by the installed validator, is structural validity, not economic approval.

**SOURCE_FACT.** [avi-mukesh's original research repository](https://github.com/avi-mukesh/crypto-quant-project) describes hourly Twitter mention counts and each coin's share of aggregate mentions. Its research uses hourly Binance observations for eight coins in 2022–2024 (BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, BNB, LINK, SHIB, DOGE), expanding to 17 coins for January 2025–May 2026. The author separately considers taker imbalance and price momentum.

**AUTHOR_REPORTED_TEST_UNVERIFIED.** The README reports relative tweet-attention features at a seven-hour lag have yearly cross-sectional IC **0.012–0.016**. This is predictive association, not a realized basket return. The author's **primary tradable portfolio is price momentum** (long/short roughly half the eligible universe with BTC regime management), **not** a proven standalone tweet-share strategy.

**LOCAL_INFERENCE.** Attention movement could precede repricing, but a tiny reported rank correlation can be erased by turnover and delays. The evaluation universe selected using May 2026 capitalization may contain hindsight membership bias; historical Twitter counts may not have been collectable at the claimed timestamp.

**ADAPTATION_PROPOSAL (separate from author).** Later investigation could define a lagged-attention cross-sectional basket for 1h decisions using strictly as-of collected counts, but no weights, cutoffs, turnover or hourly order clock are invented here.

**Falsifier:** incremental after-cost relative return over matched price momentum disappears after strict as-of historical Twitter data and universe reconstruction. No empirical tests were run.
