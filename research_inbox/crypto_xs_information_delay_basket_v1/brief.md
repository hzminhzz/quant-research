## 1. Executive Thesis

Macro news and institutional capital flows clear first in Bitcoin, diffusing sluggishly to the altcoin cross-section due to fragmented retail attention; a dollar-neutral 48h basket longing altcoins with the highest positive uncompleted information backlog from lagged Bitcoin returns and shorting negative backlog altcoins captures systematic lead-lag catch-up alpha.

This strategy targets Top 40 liquid perpetuals (ex-BTC) + BTCUSDT_PERP benchmark with a 48-Hour Rebalanced Basket execution cycle. Grounded in empirical asset pricing and market microstructure, the strategy harvests a persistent structural risk premium while mitigating directional market exposure.

## 4. Economic Mechanism and Counterparty

* **Economic Mechanism:** Grounded in Hou & Moskowitz (2005), institutional macroeconomic price discovery occurs instantaneously in Bitcoin, while altcoins incorporate market-wide shocks with significant friction and delay; longing high positive information backlog and shorting negative information backlog altcoins in a 48h dollar-neutral basket captures post-shock information diffusion catch-up drift.
* **Counterparty Payer:** Inattentive altcoin market makers and retail participants trading stale quotes who are adversely selected as macro information finishes diffusing.
* **Persistence Rationale:** Structural market segmentation: institutional ETF and CME liquidity is concentrated in Bitcoin, creating persistent information transmission lags to altcoin derivatives.
