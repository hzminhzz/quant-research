## SOURCE_FACT
Cyril Dujava, Quantpedia (7 October 2026): https://quantpedia.com/trading-the-multi-asset-drift-around-u-s-elections/

The article studies calendar sessions associated with historical US federal elections. D0 is the event session and D+1 the next US trading session. It describes a seven-series, equal-weight D+1 short exposure to XLK-SPY, EEM-SPY, IYR-SPY, HYG, GLD, USO and UDN. The three equity relative-return series require paired hedge legs. It also investigates an IEF duration rotation separately.
