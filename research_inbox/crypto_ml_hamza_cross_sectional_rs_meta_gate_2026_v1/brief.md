# QuantAlgo2: weekly crypto relative-strength basket with a LightGBM meta-gate

**THESIS_CAPTURED · SOURCE_CAPTURE_ONLY.** Original sources: [author-owned repository](https://github.com/hamzaalisayeed-eng/QuantAlgo2), [RS strategy configuration](https://github.com/hamzaalisayeed-eng/QuantAlgo2/blob/main/config/strategies/cross_sectional_rs.yaml), [model configuration](https://github.com/hamzaalisayeed-eng/QuantAlgo2/blob/main/config/meta_model.yaml) and [author's August 2026 research note](https://github.com/hamzaalisayeed-eng/QuantAlgo2/blob/main/docs/STRATEGY_RESEARCH.md). Read for this capture on 2026-10-18; no source code was executed.

The author describes 1h price data for BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and DOGE. A rule ranks 168-bar relative strength weekly; the configuration selects top-1 long and bottom-1 short, with deliberately wide ±12 ATR triple-barrier labels and a 168-bar vertical limit. A LightGBM meta-classifier with calibrated predicted trade-win probability filters primary trades but does not choose direction. The research system's actual spot deployment is **long-only**, so shorts may be discarded; the configured theoretical pair must not be presented as realized dollar-neutral performance.

**Source-reported evidence, NOT independent replication.** The author states that meta-labeling helped relative strength, but explicitly labels absolute performance as in-sample educational estimates and documents numerous other variants destroyed by ~0.30% round-trip costs. Trend filters can also screen out rare large winners. There is no verified live profit, ML OOF artifact or prop-firm transfer. The model configuration's 24-bar generic embargo needs a separate audit against this strategy's 168-bar outcome window.

**Novelty:** Conditional *trade-event reliability prediction* applied to a fixed weekly crypto rank rule. It is different from general direct-price forecasting/return-ranking or a breakout-only filter. A fair future null is unfiltered RS on the same five-coin universe with identical event/short rules, sample clocks and net costs. It is **BLOCKED_PROTOCOL** for the current ATS cross-sectional score/allocator until a qualified event-specific meta filter and OOF training chronology exist. Funding alpha from unrelated repository modules is excluded.
