## SOURCE_FACT
Cesar Alvarez, *Volume and Mean Reversion*, 2022-12-07: https://alvarezquanttrading.com/blog/volume-and-mean-reversion/. Source locations: "The Rules", "Adding Finding to a Portfolio", "Final Thoughts".

The open-study signal used Russell 3000 stocks with RSI(2)<1, close>$3, 21-day dollar-volume average >$1m, separate above/below 200-day moving-average cohorts, buy next open and sell next open after RSI(2)>70. He examined same-day rolling **PercentRank(volume,20)** and preferred very low percentile volume. His separate private portfolio application is NOT fully disclosed.

## AUTHOR_REPORTED_UNVERIFIED
Author-reported above-SMA 0–39 volume percentile setups averaged 0.64% per trade versus 0.54% unconditioned, but retained only 18% of trades. In a different portfolio application the strict <20 filter cut exposure from 25% to 5%. Rank preference gave only minor improvement; author decided the rule was not worth adopting. These returns omit independent execution assessment.

## LOCAL_INFERENCE
Lower participation during an oversold signal might identify less persistent forced selling rather than informative price discovery.
