## Original hypothesis
FactorVAE, a variational-autoencoder latent factor method, is adapted from equities to 50 cryptocurrencies using price-volume technical indicators. The author claims 2019–2024 data, OOS 2024, and seven distinct directional, market-neutral, volatility-adjusted or momentum-filtered strategy variants. Source abstract reports six positive Sharpe above 1 and a momentum-filter variant Sharpe 2.12 with 269.8% return. These are unverified reported results and are **not** grounds for promotion.

## Why interesting, and fair null
Learned latent dynamics are economically distinct from static hand-selected rank factors, but they may be expensive re-encodings of straightforward momentum and shared beta. The first actual test, separately authorized, needs PIT membership, train-only model and scaler fits, chronological OOF inference, explicit source variant chosen before results, and comparison to momentum, PCA and simpler relative-return ranking under identical risk/costs.
