# Liquidity × risk × past-return interactions — source-only draft

**THESIS_CAPTURED.** This is original-source descriptive research, not a validated native capture or scientific success.

**Source:** Aleksander Mercik, Sitara Karim, Barbara Będowska-Sójka and Adam Zaremba, *Cross-sectional interactions in cryptocurrency returns*, International Review of Financial Analysis 97 (2025), 103809. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.irfa.2024.103809 ; open manuscript metadata: https://open.icm.edu.pl/handle/123456789/25545 .

## Original claims and method
The institutional abstract describes double sorting on 40 crypto characteristics across more than 500 coins/tokens in 2017–2023, and an OOS long/short strategy selecting strongest and weakest characteristic interactions. Its **claimed** Sharpe above one is SOURCE_REPORTED_UNVERIFIED and must not be interpreted as local validation. The primary interactions combine past returns, liquidity and risk. The authors explicitly discuss the role of illiquid coins and elevated trading costs.

**Not source-extracted:** exact interaction index, lookbacks, prior selection windows, transaction cost assumptions, long/short gross weights, realized eligible constituent history, and paper's precise validation design. No trial or backtest performed.

## Novelty and caveats
Unlike generic momentum, size or illiquidity, the mechanism is *non-additive characteristic interaction*. However, the present 69-candidate corpus already has many constituent standalone factors. Treat the source as a genuinely distinct family only if the full text confirms source-fixed double-sort definitions and interaction rankings. There is significant data-mining and hard-to-trade-small-cap risk; an OOS result alone does not establish future robustness.

## Prop portability / unknown
Original source coin sample does not establish exchange-perpetual eligibility, crypto-prop listing, shorting/netting or hedging permissions. Accounting for financing costs remains mandatory in any separately approved backtest; financing is not a signal.
