## 1. Executive Thesis

Altcoins with high idiosyncratic volatility relative to Bitcoin are systematically overpriced by retail lottery demand and limits of arbitrage; a dollar-neutral basket longing low-IVOL and shorting high-IVOL tokens earns substantial risk-adjusted alpha.

This strategy targets Top 40 liquid perpetuals (ex-BTC) + BTCUSDT_PERP benchmark with a 72-Hour Rebalanced Basket execution cycle. Grounded in empirical asset pricing and market microstructure, the strategy harvests a persistent structural risk premium while mitigating directional market exposure.

## 4. Economic Mechanism and Counterparty

* **Economic Mechanism:** Grounded in Ang et al. (2006) and Stambaugh et al. (2015), retail lottery preferences and short-sale arbitrage asymmetry bid up high idiosyncratic volatility altcoins, causing subsequent underperformance; going Long low-IVOL and Short high-IVOL tokens in a dollar-neutral 72h basket captures the structural overvaluation discount.
* **Counterparty Payer:** Risk-averse hedgers, noise traders, and forced margin liquidators.
* **Persistence Rationale:** Structural market frictions, margin limits to arbitrage, and non-diversifiable risk constraints.
