# nber_eu_ets_april_compliance_liquidity_202606_v1 — EU carbon compliance pressure

## SOURCE_FACT
[NBER Working Paper 35356 (June 2026)](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35356), Nicola Borri, Yukun Liu, Aleh Tsyvinski and Xi Wu; also [arXiv 2606.03767](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03767). The **EU ETS emission allowance** study covers **2.7 million registry transactions from 2005–2021**. Authors report about **40% of operators do not trade in a year**, purchases concentrate in **April**, and returns are systematically elevated in that compliance period.

## AUTHOR_REPORTED_UNVERIFIED
Operators' flows reportedly predict future allowance returns. The model links costly access, constrained intermediaries and heterogeneous information to surrender-month price premia. These claims are reported by authors; they are not a reproduced tradable after-cost strategy.

## LOCAL_INFERENCE
Mandatory compliance buyers may be price-insensitive close to a known deadline. The calendar is potentially public, but historical registry *individual operator flow* might be visible only ex post. That distinction governs whether the mechanism can yield a causal signal.

## ADAPTATION_PROPOSAL — NOT AUTHOR RULES
A future investigator could compare an accurately versioned EU ETS compliance calendar with matched non-April months, using EUA price/energy controls and actual execution costs. ICE EUA futures or CFDs demand separate contract-identity, carry, basis and expiry proofs. Do not invent a fixed long-entry date or take the registry flow as real-time.

## UNKNOWN / ADVERSE
Original effect magnitude and return clocks; time when operator transactions were disseminated; different current EU ETS regulation/compliance regime; trading spreads and rollover costs. Seasonal returns may be prepriced. No executable entry, stop, size or approved ATS protocol.
