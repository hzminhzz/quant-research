# High-frequency crypto co-jump clustering (risk evidence, not a strategy)

**Status:** SOURCE_DRAFT_UNVERIFIED and NOT EXECUTABLE. Descriptive research-only observation.

**Primary:** Saef, Nagy, Sizov & Härdle, *Understanding temporal dynamics of jumps in cryptocurrency markets: evidence from tick-by-tick data*, Digital Finance (August 8, 2024), https://doi.org/10.1007/s42521-024-00116-1. **Correction issued May 6, 2025:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s42521-025-00131-w; inspect before technical reproduction.

**Original finding:** On 2019-04-12 to 2021-09-27 ticks for six USDT-quoted major coins across seven exchanges, noise-robust jump tests detect clustering and co-occurrence. The same-day conditional co-jump association involving Bitcoin and Ethereum does NOT establish tradable BTC-leads-altcoin timing and no specific long/short basket is published. Author observations, not independent validations.

**Non-empirical boundary:** Required raw tick stream unavailable from broad Binance hourly OHLCV alone. The paper does not specify rank weights, long/short entry/exit or margin. All such details UNKNOWN; do not manufacture. Same-day conditional probabilities cannot be used as a causal entry signal without event-level sequencing.

**Novelty and adverse evidence:** Unlike preexisting *hourly* signed-jump variation feature ideas, source directly analyzes microstructure-noise-adjusted *tick-level* jump co-occurrence across venues. The authors also note BTC/ETH dominance and liquidity obstacles to studying exact altcoin jump sequences. No funding or basis alpha. Prop-firm account and instrument permissions UNKNOWN.
