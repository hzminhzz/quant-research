# bis_offshore_ndf_demand_onshore_spillover_20261016_v1

## SOURCE_FACT
[BIS Working Paper 1383, 16 October 2026](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1383-offshore-forwards-and-exchange-rate-transmission-emerging-markets). Ferrara, Schrimpf, Viswanath-Natraj and Wang use signed LCH ForexClear offshore NDF clearing flow in ten emerging-market currencies and compare offshore forward and onshore spot exchange rates.

## AUTHOR_REPORTED_UNVERIFIED
A USD 1bn offshore **long-USD** flow is associated with ~10 bps **onshore currency depreciation** and ~18 bps **offshore NDF** response within minutes. Dealer balance-sheet constraints strengthen spot impact; China, Malaysia and India capital-control changes alter transmission. These are market-impact estimates, **not a strategy PnL**. BIS summary focus describes data from 2014-2025 while its abstract says 2013-2025; exact sample needs resolution.

## LOCAL_INFERENCE
Cross-market lead-lag is conceivable only if signed offshore flow becomes visible before onshore repricing, which is unverified and may be impossible with clearing data.

## ADAPTATION_PROPOSAL
Future observational work might compare actual as-of signed-flow forecasts with quote-only benchmarks, after confirming legal feed access. No forex CFD substitution or trading rule is authorized.
