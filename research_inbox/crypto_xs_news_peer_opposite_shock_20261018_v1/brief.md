# News-driven opposite-sign peer shock and delayed reversal — source-only draft

**THESIS_CAPTURED.** Source verification only; not a native capture, executable specification, backtest or approval.

**Source:** Gustavo Schwenkler and H. Zheng, *News-driven peer co-movement in crypto markets*, Journal of Corporate Finance 93 (July 2025), article 102772. Original publisher: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcorpfin.2025.102772 . University hosted metadata: https://scholarcommons.scu.edu/finance/10/ .

## Original source facts
The authors identify cryptocurrency peers through financial-news co-mentions using NLP, rather than vendor industries. After a large idiosyncratic shock, news-linked peers exhibit abnormal contemporaneous returns of the opposite sign. The mispricing reverses over subsequent weeks, with the source conclusion describing dissipation after about three weeks. The authors describe trading strategies and return profitability; **all source performance claims remain UNVERIFIED**.

This paper documents a multiple-coin peer phenomenon, but this draft does **not** assert an exact author long/short mapping, per-asset weighting, rebalance frequency, cost assumptions or tradable crypto-perpetual list. Those details must come from original strategy text, not an adaptation.

## Discovery relevance and mechanism
Distinct from price-based correlation or peer-relative strength: the peer mapping is constructed with timestamped public news co-mentions and the predictive variable is a focal idiosyncratic shock. Strong candidate for source-deepening, not for automated execution.

## Adverse and causality concerns
Contemporaneous co-movement is not a tradeable ex-ante forecast by itself. Publication-time joins, availability of historical news, rolling co-mention peers, survivorship, lagged shock identification, and small illiquid coin execution could eliminate the apparent opportunity. Paper's returns and research replication are not independently verified.
