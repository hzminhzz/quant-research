# baldi_air_residual_shock_asymmetry_20260922_v1 — Asymmetric Reversals

## SOURCE_FACT
[NBER Working Paper 35840 (October 2026)](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35840), by Federico Baldi-Lanfranchi, Pierre Collin-Dufresne and Kent D. Daniel. [September SSRN version](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7523679). The authors divide individual-stock returns into systematic/public-news-linked SYS and orthogonal residual RES. The abstract reports SYS continuation, RES reversal, and **faster reversal following negative RES shocks** than positive ones.

## AUTHOR_REPORTED_UNVERIFIED
The AIR factor reportedly subsumes several short-horizon anomalies. No claims of independently verified returns, actual after-cost Sharpe, or capacity are made. Only official metadata and abstracts were examined; formula derivations and portfolios from the full paper remain unverified.

## LOCAL_INFERENCE
Inventory/price-pressure absorption might explain sign-dependent stock-level reversion, while systematic news continuation should not be shorted automatically.

## ADAPTATION_PROPOSAL — NOT ORIGINAL RULES
A future causal study could compare sign-conditioned RES reversal against symmetric RES reversal, using only factors and news available at the decision timestamp. Do not invent thresholds, weights, stops, bar size, position sizing or entry/exit clocks; **none is qualified here**.

## UNKNOWN / ADVERSE
Paper's exact SYS/RES estimators and returns windows, survivorship-free data, corporate actions, short-sale borrow, impact, ex-ante portfolio costs and after-period durability are unresolved. Whole-sample residual regression would leak future information. No index/CFD/crypto product substitution.
