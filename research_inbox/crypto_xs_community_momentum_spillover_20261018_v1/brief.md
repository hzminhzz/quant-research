# Crypto technology-community peer-return momentum

**THESIS_CAPTURED — SOURCE_CAPTURE_ONLY**, priority HIGH, technology_community_return_propagation. Descriptive hypothesis only, not a research/backtest/execution approval.

## Original-source evidence
Li Guo, Wolfgang Karl Härdle, Yubo Tao (2021), original: https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.11921

### SOURCE_FACT
Lagged returns of OTHER coins in dynamically clustered technology-related cryptocurrency communities may predict a coin's next relative return.

Original method: Authors build a time-varying cross-crypto network using return relations and technical/consensus characteristics and use lagged peer-community average returns to predict individual token returns; sort by expected returns.

Original long logic: Source portfolio takes top predicted-return quartile.

Original short logic: Source portfolio shorts bottom predicted-return quartile.

Source universe: Paper examines approximately 182 coins selected from the top 400 by market capitalization *as of December 2018* for historical 2016–2018 observations. That retrospective cohort creates substantial point-in-time/survivorship concerns.

Source timing: Daily forward prediction. The paper uses rolling 360-day cross-predictability estimation to build dynamic communities; exact availability/cutoff, algorithm tuning, actual exchange fill timing and execution cadence remain UNKNOWN.

Portfolio: Cross-sectional top/bottom forecast quartiles; precise weights, gross exposure and costs UNKNOWN.

### SOURCE_REPORTED_TEST — UNVERIFIED
The source reports historical empirical associations or portfolio results; these have **not** been independently replicated. We assert NO local historical profitability, Sharpe, IC, simulations or execution.

### LOCAL_INFERENCE / adverse evidence
Original sample chosen using later market-cap ranks may be survivor-biased; clustering and the coin technical-feature information clock may be noncausal.

Falsifier for any later independently registered study: Peer predictive ordering vanishes with as-of constituents and as-of tech attributes or source clustering cannot be causally reproduced.

Novelty vs the 69 prior published source folders: Other-token within-network lagged peers from time-varying technology-assisted clusters differs from simple BTC beta, whole-market momentum, static pairwise correlation or an OHLCV-only industry proxy.

### ADAPTATION_PROPOSAL — not source facts
A pure return-correlation cluster basket would NOT reproduce source CASC clusters.
