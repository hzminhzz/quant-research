# One-bar 50-breakout on the Dukascopy Nasdaq proxy

Status: `COMPLETED_EXPLORATORY`. This study does not approve trading.

## Question and scope

The hypothesis was that a stop entry at the previous 50 completed 30-minute high or low can produce positive one-bar continuation PnL. The exit rule closes the position at the opening print of the next 30-minute bar. The protective stop risks $1,000 per illustrative contract. The session limit is two filled entries per Chicago Globex session day.

The narrow falsifier was non-positive average net PnL per closed trade after the approved illustrative costs. Both prescribed trials had positive average net PnL. This is descriptive support for this observed proxy sample. It does not establish an edge on CME NQ futures.

The source is Dukascopy `usatechidxusd` (`USATECH.IDX/USD`) BID minute data. This is a Nasdaq technical-index CFD price proxy. It is not a verified CME E-mini Nasdaq-100 futures contract. The study uses one illustrative NQ-equivalent contract at $20 per index point. This maps the $1,000 stop to 50 points. The CFD quote grid and contract multiplier are not verified as CME specifications.

The study covers 2013-01-01 through the last available source date, 2026-07-14. The run did not read August 2026 or later. The input contains 163 monthly Parquet partitions and 3,883,929 rows. The naive source timestamp represents UTC. The ingestion decoder builds it from seconds after UTC midnight.

The data calendar and qualification identities were hash-verified. The NQ qualification identity is `f27123afdeeaf13b65fd1215b7fdc4c751f870a370cb0d3c91477328d08f0e95`. The Dukascopy US index calendar identity is `663481a30a405212727eb397bb8ea406c114696b2905143c01a50eb8e5b2e8dc`. The NQ instrument calendar identity is `8264e388d6002fecf2320f35b64e34afb51ece3ec8379aabc69f5bd918eb191b`. The calendar qualifies the source through 2026-08-17.

## Frozen method

The two trials were zero cost and cost aware. No strategy parameters or filters were tuned. The cost-aware trial charged $2.50 commission and 0.0625 index points of adverse slippage per side. The slippage is one quarter of the NQ 0.25-point tick. These costs are illustrative assumptions. They are not verified CFD brokerage costs.

The runner formed elapsed 30-minute UTC bars from the 1-minute source. Each Chicago session starts at 17:00 America/Chicago. This keeps bars separate through DST changes. The runner retained only bars with 30 distinct source minutes. It used the previous 50 complete bars for each stop level. Native `ml4t.backtest` STOP orders checked the 1-minute OHLC path. The runner canceled the opposite entry after the first fill. It submitted a native MARKET order before the next 30-minute boundary so the next 1-minute event filled it at that opening print. It used a $1,000 protective stop and capped fills at two per Chicago session date.

The run used `ml4t.backtest.Strategy`, `DataFeed`, and `Engine` in `NEXT_BAR` mode. The initial account balance was $1,000,000 for metrics only. The engine used a $25,000 initial and $20,000 maintenance margin schedule for one position. These values are not verified broker or exchange margin. The engine recorded 10,300 fills and 5,150 closed trades per trial. It rejected no orders.

The runner rounds stop levels to the nearest $0.25 NQ-equivalent grid point. This makes proxy execution deterministic. It does not make CFD prices CME tick-conforming.

## Source gaps and execution limits

The source audit found 121,319 complete 30-minute bars and excluded 11,744 incomplete bars. It found 24,567 timestamp gaps longer than one minute. The largest was 22,411 minutes, from 2017-01-16 17:29 UTC to 2017-02-01 07:00 UTC.

The calendar lists 4,328,253 expected open minutes in the selected date range. The source has 444,329 fewer rows than those intervals imply. It has missing rows in 1,940 of 5,771 expected-open intervals, including 362 intervals with no rows and 1,780 dates with at least one gap. Some calendar regimes cover full UTC days. This count therefore flags a calendar-to-source coverage mismatch. It does not classify every missing minute as an outage. The source quality artifact records the 20 largest interval gaps.

The runner did not forward-fill source bars. It disabled entries when it could not verify the next entry bar and its following exit opening print. It found 110,729 bars with 50 prior levels and two consecutive following 30-minute bars. A long source gap can still leave the previous 50 observed complete bars far back in time. The exact rule uses those previous complete bars. This study did not add a freshness filter.

The engine filled 3,117 entry stops at an opening price beyond the stop level. It recorded 194 protective-stop exits. Ninety-four protective stops gapped through their stop level and used the opening price. The 1-minute OHLC data cannot show the order of an entry trigger and a protective stop inside the same minute. The runner flagged 42 such cases. It did not invent an intraminute path.

All closed trades held for 30 minutes or less. No exit filled after the required next boundary. The highest entry count was two per session day. The ledgers retain native fill prices, order types, stop levels, timestamps, commission, slippage, and exit reasons.

## Interpretation and next question

The result passes the narrow positive-average-PnL falsifier under the user-specified price and cost assumptions. The calendar-to-source gaps, 42 ambiguous minute paths, unverified CFD-to-NQ mapping, and illustrative cost assumption limit the conclusion. The manager’s prior selection history is incomplete, so this study does not claim a reproducible Deflated Sharpe Ratio or a complete multiple-testing adjustment. The declared count of two refers only to this study’s zero-cost and cost-aware trials.

The next decisive question is whether this result survives an independent reconstruction on verified dated CME NQ contracts with documented rolls and instrument-specific costs. That evidence is not present here.

The first execution used local wall-clock buckets and merged the repeated fall-DST hour. The run is preserved in [`superseded_wall_clock_attempt/`](../../run_log/one_bar_dukascopy_2013_2026_20261011/superseded_wall_clock_attempt/). The corrected run uses elapsed UTC intervals. Both cost variants were rerun. This produced four engine executions for two declared variants. Trade and fill values match between both runs. The corrected trade and fill ledgers label timestamps as UTC. The daily-equity and yearly-summary ledgers are byte-identical. The final result uses the corrected run.
