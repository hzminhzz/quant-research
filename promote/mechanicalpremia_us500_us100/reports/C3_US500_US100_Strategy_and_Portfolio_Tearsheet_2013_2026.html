Position sizing · account-equity based, not fixed contract count

At each eligible entry, the model starts with a nominal risk budget equal to 1% of current simulated account equity. It allocates that budget by sleeve and instrument, and estimates position quantity from ATR distance, subject to a native notional cap and shared risk reservation. Quantity therefore changes over time; contracts are not fixed.

Standalone tests: 0.50% nominal risk budget per index for that engine. Combined test: H1 0.1168% per index and D1 0.3832% per index. The budget is a sizing rule, not a guarantee that the account cannot lose more than 1%; D1 has no hard stop.

Methodology, controls and unresolved risks

Method and validation

Native ML4T Engine backtests with registered ml4t-engineer ATR, Bollinger Bands and EMA features. H1 breakout uses 23.36% of the nominal risk budget; D1 mean reversion uses 76.64%. Each sleeve divides allocation equally between US500 and US100.

The 1% budget is based on simulated equity and estimated ATR risk distance; actual contract quantity changes with volatility, price, and exposure limits. The model assumes 100,000 starting synthetic account units for each separately evaluated calendar year (2013–2025), with 2026 ending on October 11. The +50.15% linked ROI assumes hypothetical reinvestment between annual periods; it is not a continuous account backtest. Closed-trade profit factor and win rate exclude open year-end positions.

Not certified for real money

Dukascopy CFD bid-only one-minute OHLCV is not a verified executable bid/ask feed. Missing terms include real spreads, CFD lot and tick multipliers, overnight financing and exact market session parity. The H1 initial stop is placed after observing the fill, leaving the entry minute not fully protected.

The C3 selection followed observed historical results and therefore has no pristine OOS test in this window. Keeping C3 means retaining it as a research candidate, not approving live execution.
