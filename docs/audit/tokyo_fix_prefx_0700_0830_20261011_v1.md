**Evaluation:** January 2020–August 2026 JST; protected September–October 2026 held sealed  
**Instrument:** USD/JPY LSE 1-minute OHLCV *research-price proxy*, not verified executable broker bid/ask  
**Account:** USD 10,000 initial equity, fixed USD 10,000 base notional, no compounding

## Executive decision

The fixed Gotobi long **07:00–08:30 JST** generated **+$158.72 after an assumed 1.0-pip round-trip cost** across **402** complete-session trades, or **+0.3733 net pips/trade**. The same-time ordinary-session long lost **$1,151.47** over **963** trades. The weekday/year-controlled calendar difference is positive and survives correction *within this session's eight registered contrasts*.

However, the hypothesis **fails the frozen economic stability falsifier**: 2024–2025 combined P&L is negative, with losses in 2023 and 2025. Its own unconditional net mean is not statistically distinct from zero (HAC p=0.441); its modest overall dollar return is cost-sensitive. The earlier 2020–August 2026 source data and this time window were already examined in Programs 1 and 2, so this is not independent out-of-sample evidence. Current broker cost and executable fill assumptions cannot be verified.

**Decision: No live-trading approval and no regime-selected strategy promotion.** The next decisive test, if justified, is prospective collection of broker executable quotes and verified market fills; do not consume the sealed September–October 2026 holdout for another retrospective search.

**LIMITATIONS:** Synthetic LSE OHLCV broker proxy; actual executable bid/ask, spread, slippage, commissions and margin policies UNKNOWN. The fixed USD10k trade notional equals 1× *initial* equity but may slightly exceed contemporaneous equity after losses; max observed Gotobi ratio in the primary run was 1.000073×. ## Program conclusion and decision

**Research session is complete. Deployment remains NOT_APPROVED.** It is plausible that a Gotobi-specific early flow component exists relative to ordinary mornings. But this study **cannot support a standalone trading rule** because the after-cost mean is weak, 2023 and 2025 are negative, 2024–2025 jointly fails the frozen gate, broker execution costs are not calibrated, and the history has already been studied.

A future prospective assessment should collect executable 06:59–08:30 quotes and actual market-order fills, freeze cost and entry rules, and evaluate *newly observed* sessions. Do not reclassify September–October 2026 as a clean holdout or optimize this rule on it.
