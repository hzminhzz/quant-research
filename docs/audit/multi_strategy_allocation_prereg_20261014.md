# Four-strategy ML4T allocation — preregistration v0 (2026-10-14)

**Classification:** EXPLORATORY allocation study. No promotion or live approval. All four source strategies remain PROMOTED_FOR_FURTHER_RESEARCH and NOT_APPROVED.

**Scope:** Separate capital allocation across frozen `one_bar_nq_30m` (B0, Dukascopy USATECH.IDX/USD CFD), `donchian_btc` (Binance BTCUSDT perpetual), `rsi_30_50_spy` (Dukascopy ES CFD index price proxy, NOT ETF), and `mechanicalpremia_us500_us100` (C3, Dukascopy ES/NQ CFDs, H1+D1 combined). Source strategies, execution parameters and position sizing remain frozen.

**Mechanism:** The strategies may have different volatility and correlation regimes. Cross-strategy allocation based on previously observed return history may improve net risk-adjusted performance relative to equal capital weights. No claim of new alpha from entry signals.

**Falsifier:** Reject this hypothesis if the linear allocator fails to improve net out-of-sample development Sharpe by at least 0.15 relative to monthly rebalanced equal weights, or has deeper maximum drawdown, or loses its improvement at 2× portfolio reallocation cost. Examine uncertainty with a block-bootstrap of monthly differential returns. If evidence is inconclusive, label INCONCLUSIVE, not PASS.

**Windows and holdout:** All source data for allocation development are strictly before 2025-01-01 UTC. Initial fit period 2019-09 through 2021-12 (subject to actual complete observation eligibility); expanding monthly walk-forward decisions over 2022-01 through 2024-12. Final allocator retrospective evaluation 2025-01-01 through 2026-07-14 remains LOCKED, and has already been seen during underlying strategy selection, so is not a pristine strategy-level holdout. Do not score, read, or even compute model features from that period now. Do not freeze a final-release model on already seen 2025-26 outcomes.

**Independent model and trial:** Exactly one primary linear learned allocator configuration from installed `ml4t-models==0.1.4`: `LinearFeaturePortfolioModel(LinearPortfolioConfig(ridge_alpha=1e-4, fit_intercept=True, gross_exposure=1.0, net_exposure=1.0, max_abs_weight=0.40, seed=42))`. Installed postprocessor must be used; no PyTorch. It pools strategy observations to predict NEXT month's strategy-equity return based only on features available at the monthly decision. Suggested raw features fixed now: lagged 1-month strategy return, lagged 3-month return, trailing 21 and 63 observed-session realized volatility, and trailing 63 observed-session drawdown; any later change is a new recorded trial. Training sample includes only monthly labels whose end mark is known as of the training decision.

**Monthly portfolio accounting:** Only use observed NAV snapshots from each strategy at explicitly identified *common observation / valuation cutoffs*. Native timestamps, valuation meaning, time-zone and availability lag must be verified before using common endpoints. A strategy's return across a monthly interval equals its observed marked equity at the current and previous common endpoints (ratio minus one). This inherently includes crypto weekend exposure; never insert zeros, forward-fill missing returns, or equate a strategy's close label with a different strategy's actual clock. If exact compatible endpoints do not exist, fail closed with blocker; document an audited alignment convention as a new version before fitting. Do not merge on date strings alone.

**Capital and units:** Each strategy is an already-sized synthetic account; allocation weights refer to capital committed to each *unchanged* strategy return stream. Convert each from its actual native equity ratio to a unit return only after proving native NAV unit, payout currency, leverage, and consistent sizing. Do not divide raw PnL by a shared $100,000 without validation. C3 and B0 continuous-account replays are the development sources. BTC uses its marked equity plus the documented funding-overlay correction (fixed native fills). RSI uses native marked equity. Explicit separate CFD financing, omitted bid/ask spread, liquidation, B0 intraminute ambiguity, and funding proxy sensitivity remain caveats; historical research claims only.

**Baselines:** (a) full cash at 0% nominal yield; (b) 25% of allocation NAV in each unchanged strategy, monthly rebalanced; (c) 63-observed-session inverse-volatility weighted allocation with 40% cap, monthly rebalanced and no lookahead. Learned strategy weights constrained to [0,0.40] each and sum to 1; NO defensive cash sleeve for learned allocator in v0, cash used only for baseline (changing this is another trial).

**Allocation costs:** Source-native execution costs are retained in strategy NAV. Additional turnover of strategy-capital allocations carries predeclared *scenario* cost = 10 bps per dollar of one-way transferred notional, where one-way turnover is 0.5 × sum(abs(new_weight - pretrade_weight)). Include 1×, 2× and 3× costs, and account for portfolio-weight drift between rebalance dates. This is a hypothetical friction, not certified CFD broker costs; no live readiness claim.

**Metrics and decision:** report annualized Sharpe and its monthly aggregation assumption, CAGR, maximum drawdown, Calmar, Sortino, turnover, concentration, drawdown duration, return-vs-cash, and net Sharpe differential vs equal weights with block bootstrap CI (>=3-month blocks). Declare complete trial count (including failures) and do not tune hyperparameters after viewing development results to rescue the thesis. No repeated final holdout access. Record provenance hashes, ML4T package versions, split boundaries, feature availability, model coefficient identity, and execution assumptions.

**Fail-closed prerequisites before any model fit:**
1. Prove four development equity accounts have compatible economics and currencies; record capital unit, execution cost/funding inclusions, leverage and broker approximation caveats.
2. Verify common real valuation cutoffs or a rigorously causal mapping; no date-label join without clock reconciliation.
3. Reconcile source equity identities and zero/missing distinction; demonstrate no 2025+ source outcomes are read.
4. Ensure observed monthly endpoints and >=24 pretrain monthly samples (otherwise model underpowered and cannot fit responsibly).
5. Test installed `PortfolioSequenceBatch` feature/return shape, model postprocessor, and next-month label timing on synthetic fixtures only.
6. Write source manifest and trial registry before fitting.
