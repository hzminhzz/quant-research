## Verified defects and decisions

1. Original `src/synthesis.py` supplied invented comparison Sharpes and assigned
   DSR=1 to a positive single-trial Sharpe. These are removed; insufficient scalar
   history now produces INCONCLUSIVE rather than fictitious selection evidence.
2. Original `src/pipeline.py` treated seven completed stages as production readiness
   and called a handcrafted simulator in `src/backtest.py`. The old aggregate
   runner is blocked; stage count never grants approval. The new synthetic route
   uses the existing native execution and independent fill audit, not a second
   simulation engine. Historical stage helpers remain legacy-only until migration.
3. Generic research_control still contains five strategy/vendor implementations.
   Their relocation and caller migration are NOT completed. A rename without
   source/contract/import/fill parity is not sufficient evidence for deletion.
4. Event/availability clocks alone do not represent revision history. The bounded
   certifier binds vintage/recorded clocks where revised sources are declared,
   supported exchange-calendar schedules and exact timezone bytes. A certificate
   cannot guarantee all future runs or enforce physical I/O access by itself.
5. Candle/tick-count data cannot substantiate genuine exchange auction, traded
   volume-profile or order-book claims. New helpers label signed-volume and
   semivariance-difference quantities as proxies and reject unsupported capabilities.
6. Statistical diagnostics cannot authorize holdout access or deployment. Current
   oracle gaps are explicit INCONCLUSIVE reasons, even with perfect scores.

## Ranked architecture work

**First: complete one certified native strategy execution path.** Reuse the current
session/permit/attempt owners, extract one real strategy behind StrategyPlugin,
validate its declarative config before opening scoped data, and run the same
native fill audit. This gives the highest locality and leverage without creating
another workflow engine. Migrate the next strategy only after this route is proved.

**Second: bind statistical diagnostics to immutable evidence.** Build a derived
index over existing append-only attempts/trials, retain failures and raw trial
counts, require aligned comparable returns, reconstruct actual CPCV OOS return
paths, and bind the IC/net-Sharpe null and chronological validation. Existing
oracle callbacks and identity strings do not establish this historical coverage.

**Third: connect pure risk budgeting to an audited adapter.** Keep the policy free
of broker side effects. Provide calibrated probability/payoff evidence, current
and prospective-midnight account state, conservative stress costs and existing
aggregate risk. Treat sizing as a bounded heuristic, not Bellman optimality.

## Scientific corrections to the requested specification

- Labels intentionally use future outcomes; isolate them from decision features
  and enforce training cutoffs/purge intervals. Do not prohibit their future data
  in a way that makes labeling impossible.
- Keep chronological validation alongside CPCV. For N groups and k test groups,
  split count is C(N,k) and path count is C(N-1,k-1). Segment incidence is not a
  completed backtest-return distribution.
- Target-block permutation and block bootstrap are different null generators.
  The present diagnostic permutes equal contiguous target blocks without
  replacement and refits the supplied callback; the validity of exchangeability
  and the full market research null remain unqualified.
- Native effective-rank K_eff is not literally eigenvalue clustering. Preserve
  method identity and all attempted hypotheses; correlation cannot erase missing
  searches. DSR is not a posterior guarantee of future profit.
- The implemented illustrative 2-Step daily floor uses Prague-midnight balance
  minus 5% of initial capital, not an intraday equity high-water mark. Its overall
  floor is static. Product-specific account rules must be checked separately.
- Nonzero exposure under unbounded gaps cannot guarantee zero breach probability.
  The bounded fractional-Kelly taper is explicitly not a solved Bellman policy.
